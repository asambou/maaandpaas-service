About Maa & Paa's Homecare Service

Comfort. Care. Companionship — we care like family.

OUR STORY

We treat every client like our own family

Maa & Paa's Homecare Service was founded to give elderly people the assistance they need with daily living, without having to leave the comfort of their own homes. Based in Sanchaba Sulay Jobe, The Gambia, our team of nurses and trained caregivers works closely with families to provide safe, dignified, and compassionate in-home support.

  • ✓
    Compassion first
    Every visit is guided by empathy and respect for our clients' dignity.
  • ✓
    Trained & qualified staff
    Our caregivers and nurses are trained to support daily living and monitor health.
  • ✓
    Family-centred care
    We work with families to build a care plan suited to each individual.
