1. Purpose of this Agreement

1.1 Maas and Paas Home Care agrees to provide home-care and support services to the Client at the address stated above.

1.2 The purpose of the service is to support the Client to remain as safe, independent, dignified and comfortable as reasonably possible in their own home.

1.3 The services provided will be based on the Client's assessed needs and the agreed Care Plan.

1.4 The Client will receive a copy of their Care Plan and any significant changes to the Care Plan will be discussed with the Client and/or their authorised representative where appropriate.

2. Services Provided

Subject to the Client's assessed needs, agreed Care Plan and the Provider's legal and regulatory permissions, services may include:

  • Personal care and assistance with personal hygiene.
  • Assistance with dressing and undressing.
  • Assistance with toileting and continence care.
  • Assistance with eating and drinking.
  • Preparation of meals and drinks.
  • Assistance with mobility and transfers where appropriately assessed.
  • Medication support where legally permitted and included within the Care Plan.
  • Domestic support directly connected with the Client's care.
  • Companionship and social support.
  • Assistance with appointments and agreed activities.
  • Monitoring and reporting changes in the Client's wellbeing.
  • Other agreed services recorded in the Care Plan.

The Provider will not undertake tasks outside its employees' competence, training, assessment or legal/regulatory authority.

3. Care Plan and Assessment

3.1 Before care begins, Maas and Paas Home Care will carry out an appropriate assessment of the Client's needs.

3.2 The assessment will consider, where relevant:

  • Personal care requirements.
  • Mobility and falls risks.
  • Communication needs.
  • Nutrition and hydration.
  • Medication support.
  • Mental capacity and consent.
  • Safeguarding concerns.
  • Environmental and health-and-safety risks.
  • Equipment required for safe care.
  • Emergency arrangements.
  • Preferences, cultural requirements and personal choices.

3.3 A Care Plan will be prepared based on the assessment.

3.4 The Care Plan will be reviewed periodically and whenever there is a significant change in the Client's needs.

3.5 The Client and/or their authorised representative should inform Maas and Paas Home Care promptly of any material change in the Client's health, circumstances, medication, mobility, living arrangements or care requirements.

4. Client Rights and Dignity

Maas and Paas Home Care will seek to provide services in a manner that respects the Client's:

  • Dignity.
  • Privacy.
  • Independence.
  • Choice.
  • Personal preferences.
  • Confidentiality.
  • Cultural and religious needs.
  • Right to make decisions about their own care where they have capacity to do so.

The Provider will not discriminate unlawfully against a Client, employee or other person.

5. Consent and Mental Capacity

5.1 Care will normally be provided with the Client's consent.

5.2 Where there are concerns about the Client's ability to make a particular decision, Maas and Paas Home Care will follow applicable legal requirements and its policies concerning mental capacity and best-interest decision-making.

5.3 The Provider will not assume that a person lacks capacity simply because of their age, disability, illness or diagnosis.

5.4 Where appropriate, the Provider may communicate with a legally authorised representative, attorney, deputy or other person with appropriate authority.

6. Staff

6.1 Maas and Paas Home Care will take reasonable steps to ensure that care workers are appropriately recruited, trained, supervised and competent for the duties they undertake.

6.2 The Provider may change the care worker assigned to the Client where reasonably necessary, including because of sickness, holidays, staffing requirements, safeguarding concerns or operational circumstances.

6.3 The Provider will endeavour to maintain continuity of care where reasonably practicable.

6.4 The Client and their family must treat care workers with dignity and respect. Threatening, abusive, discriminatory or violent behaviour will not be accepted.

7. Safeguarding

7.1 Maas and Paas Home Care has a zero-tolerance approach to abuse, neglect, exploitation and mistreatment.

7.2 The Provider will follow applicable safeguarding procedures and may report concerns to appropriate authorities where required.

7.3 The Provider may take immediate reasonable action where it believes the Client or another person is at risk of serious harm.

7.4 Confidentiality will not prevent the Provider from sharing information where disclosure is required or permitted by law, necessary to protect someone from serious harm, or required by a regulatory or safeguarding authority.

8. Medication

8.1 Medication assistance will only be provided where it is included within the Client's Care Plan and can lawfully and safely be undertaken by the relevant care worker.

8.2 The Provider will follow its medication policies and applicable professional, legal and regulatory requirements.

8.3 The Client and/or their representative must inform the Provider of relevant changes to prescribed medication.

8.4 Maas and Paas Home Care will document medication support and report medication-related incidents or concerns in accordance with its procedures.

11. Emergencies and Changes in Condition

11.1 If a care worker reasonably believes that the Client is experiencing a medical or other emergency, the care worker will seek appropriate emergency assistance.

11.2 The Provider will notify the appropriate family member, representative or professional where appropriate and permitted.

11.3 The Client and/or representative must notify the Provider of significant changes to the Client's condition or circumstances.

11.4 Maas and Paas Home Care may request a reassessment where the Client's needs have changed significantly.

12. Health and Safety

12.1 The Provider will undertake appropriate risk assessments relating to the care it provides.

12.2 The Client agrees to provide reasonable access to the property and to inform the Provider about known hazards that could affect the Client or care workers.

12.3 The Provider may suspend or alter a service where the environment presents a serious and immediate safety risk, while taking reasonable steps to protect the Client and arrange appropriate support.

13. Access to the Client's Home

13.1 The Client agrees to provide care workers with reasonable access to the property at agreed visit times.

13.2 Where keys, access codes or other access arrangements are required, these will be recorded appropriately.

13.3 The Provider will take reasonable steps to protect keys and access information.

13.4 The Client should not request a care worker to undertake tasks that are outside the agreed Care Plan, their competence or their legal responsibilities.

14. Confidentiality and Data Protection

14.1 Maas and Paas Home Care will process personal information in accordance with applicable data-protection legislation.

14.2 Personal information will only be accessed, used or disclosed for legitimate purposes connected with providing care, managing the service, complying with legal obligations, protecting individuals or otherwise as permitted by law.

14.3 The Provider will maintain appropriate records relating to the Client's care.

14.4 The Client may have rights concerning their personal information under applicable data-protection law.

Maas and Paas Home Care will maintain appropriate records relating to the care provided, including relevant care notes, incidents, safeguarding concerns, medication records and reviews where applicable. Records will be maintained securely and retained in accordance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

17. Insurance and Liability

17.1 Maas and Paas Home Care will maintain appropriate insurance for its business and services, as required by law and appropriate to its activities.

17.2 Nothing in this Agreement excludes or limits liability where such exclusion or limitation would be unlawful.

17.3 The Provider will provide services with reasonable care and skill as required by applicable law.

17.4 The Client should notify the Provider as soon as reasonably possible of any incident, loss, damage or concern relating to the services.

19. Termination

19.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving: Notice period: __________________ days.

19.4 Nothing in this clause permits the Provider to terminate services in a manner that breaches applicable law or regulatory requirements.

20. Changes to this Agreement

20.1 Any material change to the agreed services, fees or terms will be communicated clearly to the Client.

20.2 Changes will take effect only in accordance with applicable law and after any required notice or agreement has been provided.

20.3 The Provider will not rely on hidden or unfair contractual terms.

21. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the law of the Republic of The Gambia, unless another jurisdiction is expressly and lawfully agreed. Any dispute will first be addressed through the Provider's complaints procedure where appropriate. Nothing in this clause prevents either party from exercising any statutory rights or seeking appropriate legal remedies.

22. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with the Client's Care Plan and any documents expressly incorporated into it, records the agreed arrangements between the parties concerning the provision of the services. If there is a conflict between this Agreement and a mandatory legal or regulatory requirement, the legal or regulatory requirement will take precedence.
