FEATURED PARTNER

Supporting GOVI — The Gambia Organisation of the Visually Impaired

GOVI is a national, registered non-governmental organisation established in 1991 to promote the rights, dignity, independence and inclusion of persons with visual impairment in The Gambia. It works across 10 branches and serves more than 3,000 members, with a strong focus on education, skills development, economic empowerment, advocacy, human rights and social inclusion.

GOVI operates a School for the Blind, providing free education and comprehensive support to visually impaired children and young people — including learning materials, transportation, feeding and medical assistance. Through its Women's and Youth Wings and community-based programmes, GOVI empowers visually impaired women and youth to participate meaningfully in society, access opportunities, claim their rights and become self-reliant.

Maa & Paa's Homecare is proud to support GOVI's mission. Every contribution helps GOVI expand its programmes, strengthen its capacity and reach more visually impaired people — particularly women, youth and children.

  • Established in 1991, working across 10 branches nationwide
  • Serves more than 3,000 members across The Gambia
  • Runs a free School for the Blind — learning materials, transport, feeding & medical support
  • Women's & Youth Wings empowering visually impaired women and young people
